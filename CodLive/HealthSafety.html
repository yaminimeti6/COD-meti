Health & Safety Instructions

your safety is important to us. Please read the following instructions carefully before using any of our products.

1. Medical Consultation

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before use if you are:

  • Pregnant or nursing
  • Planning to conceive
  • Suffering from any medical condition, including but not limited to heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid disorders, liver or kidney conditions
  • Taking prescription medications, especially blood thinners, antidepressants, stimulants, hormone therapy, or diabetes medication

Individuals with known allergies or sensitivities should carefully review ingredient lists before use.

2. Recommended Usage

  • Use only as directed on the product label.
  • Do not exceed the recommended dosage.
  • Overconsumption may cause adverse effects.

3. Age Restriction

Not intended for individuals under 18 years of age unless specifically advised by a qualified healthcare professional.

4. Allergen Warning

  • Please review the ingredient list carefully before use.
  • If you have known allergies, consult your doctor prior to consumption.
  • Products may contain or be manufactured in facilities that handle common allergens.

5. Possible Side Effects

Discontinue use immediately and consult a healthcare professional if you experience:

  • Rapid heartbeat
  • Shortness of breath
  • Severe headache
  • Rash or allergic reaction
  • Dizziness
  • Nausea or unusual discomfort
  • Any unexpected symptoms

6. Storage Instructions

  • Store in a cool, dry place.
  • Keep away from direct sunlight and moisture.
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets.
  • Do not use if the seal is broken or damaged.
